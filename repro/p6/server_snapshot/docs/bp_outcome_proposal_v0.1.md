# P6 proposed BP outcome rule v0.1

Status: DEVELOPMENT ONLY - NOT FROZEN

Proposed choices, not a CDC-mandated algorithm:
- Use conventional BPX readings 1-3 only.
- Require at least two complete systolic/diastolic pairs.
- Average each component over the same retained pairs.
- Provisionally retain zero diastolic values; review before freezing.
- Positive: mean systolic >=130 OR mean diastolic >=80 mmHg.
- Negative: both means below threshold.
- Insufficient pairs: outcome unavailable, not negative.
- Do not impute missing outcomes.

Single-examination screening phenotype, not confirmed clinical diagnosis.
Measurement validity and comparison with NHANES averaging procedures remain unresolved.
No cohort construction or modelling authorized.
